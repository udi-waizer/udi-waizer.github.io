Path to
solution

Single Pane of Glass

The world of Data Science is complicated...like, really complicated! Many types of 'personas' and users, wide array of use-case and too many IDE-like solutions which overlap and maintain a high level of experience-complexity in an already dense domain.

In order to be a positive disrupter, we focused on learning how our audience works, highlighting main pain points; and instead of being, yet another player solving part of the problem - we designed and built a feature-rich platform that enables to manage the full range of data science projects, highly pluggable, collaborative, simplistic and user-empowering!

Design
work

Zepl introduced a new standard in the market for Data Science teams, allowing for quick and easy data-connectivity and rapid processing.

Snowflake integration and partnership enabled a hyper-growth and access to larger clients; the platform’s next generation UI/UX made for a simplistic, intuitive and empowering tool for team leaders and data workers.
